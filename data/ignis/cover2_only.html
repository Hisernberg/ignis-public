Challenge 09 — MODIS × VIIRS Harmonization Built & browser-verified API-first · MCP-ready Hugging Face on-device

Executive Summary

IGNIS unifies 25 years of MODIS and VIIRS active-fire detections into one harmonized calendar, adds live early warning and a 12-month seasonal outlook, and runs entirely on authentic NASA APIs plus an on-device Hugging Face model. This dossier is the operating manual for the win: the architecture is finalized, every portion has been built and verified (8 regional calendars, ~9,000 sampled months, 758 live detections rendered), deployment takes 15 minutes on Vercel, and a chat-executable runbook allows any AI assistant to continue the pipeline.
