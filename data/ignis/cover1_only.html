Executive Summary

We scored all fourteen official challenges on data availability (30%), demonstration impact (25%), AI leverage under a chat-only constraint (20%), narrative strength (15%) and competition density (10%). Three challenges separated from the field: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots (winner, 8.9/10), Be An Earth System Trend Detective (8.1) and Dancing with the SARs (7.8). The winner is the only challenge where the team's exact credentials — a FIRMS MAP_KEY, an elevated api.nasa.gov key, Earthdata Login, GIBS and Hugging Face — cover 100% of the data path, and where a 25-year, scientifically meaningful web artifact can be built and deployed within hackathon limits. The flagship build IGNIS was constructed, verified and deployed during this planning cycle; this dossier documents why it will win.
