SECTION 07 // EXECUTION
Pre-Mission Timeline — Ten Weeks Out

The weekly training schedule that gets you to November 14 with roughly seventy percent of the flagship already built and drilled.

The gap between a Global Nominee and a good local project is almost never talent — it is what was finished before the clock started. This schedule assumes a 2–4 person team investing roughly 6–10 hours per week alongside work or study. Weeks are deliberately themed: build the muscles in order, and do not skip the rehearsal weeks, because they are where the demo confidence that wins judges is actually forged. If you discover this plan later than ten weeks out, compress the early weeks but never cut Weeks 8–9.

Ten focused weeks — roughly 80 team-hours — buys you a 48-hour event where 70% of the work is done, the AI is already deployed, and your only real-time job is adaptation and polish. That is the entire competitive edge of this playbook, stated as arithmetic.
SECTION 08 // EXECUTION
The 48-Hour Battle Plan

Six phases, three go/no-go gates, and a triage ladder — the hour-by-hour doctrine for event weekend.

The battle plan assumes the flagship chassis from Section 06 and the ten-week prep from Section 07. Its structure follows a simple asymmetric logic: front-load decisions, back-load polish. The first two hours choose the target and the mission skin; the middle phases execute adaptation; the final third of the clock belongs entirely to the demo, the video, and the submission — the artifacts judges actually touch. Each phase ends with a go/no-go gate where the team consciously decides what drops and what continues.

H+0 — H+2PHASE 1 // TARGET LOCK
  • H+0: Read the full challenge list individually. Each member scores every plausible challenge alone, in silence, using the Section 5 matrix.
  • H+1: Compare scores; discuss only the top two. Apply weights, resolve ties via captain. Announce the pick and stop discussing it.
  • H+2: Write the one-sentence mission statement and the single persona it serves. Pin it in the team channel; every later decision cites it.
GATE 1: CHALLENGE LOCKED + MISSION STATEMENT WRITTEN. NO REOPENING THE DEBATE WITHOUT CAPTAIN OVERRIDE.
H+2 — H+12PHASE 2 // RE-SKIN THE CHASSIS
  • Rewrite the challenge-specific surfaces: feed filters, map layers, console labels, hero copy. The codebase underneath does not change.
  • Swap the AI plan column from Section 5.2 into the pipeline config; point the briefer’s context builder at the challenge’s primary dataset.
  • Split now: strongest frontend hand drives the re-skin; second builder wires challenge data into the pipeline; ML lead validates models on challenge data and drafts the validation metric.
  • Begin the project page skeleton in parallel — never leave it for the end.
GATE 2 (H+12): THE APP LOOKS LIKE IT ANSWERS THIS CHALLENGE. FIRST LIVE WALKTHROUGH OF THE FULL DEMO PATH.
H+12 — H+24PHASE 3 // AI ONLINE
  • Integrate the three chosen models into the live path (default doctrine: Chronos forecast, retrieval, briefer). Validate outputs on real challenge data; print one metric per model.
  • Build the Data Ledger page and per-screen source attributions — an hour of work that pays for itself at every judging level.
  • Deploy to the public URL. From this hour forward, the link must stay alive; every commit ships behind it.
H+24 — H+36PHASE 4 // SHARPEN & HARDEN
  • Story Mode gets its final cut. Loading states, empty states, error states for the entire demo path — judges click sideways.
  • Apply the first triage pass: any feature more than 50% unfinished at H+30 is formally cut without debate. scope discipline is a feature.
  • Write the science paragraph for the project page: method, assumptions, validation numbers, dataset versions. This is your Best Use of Science armor.
GATE 3 (H+36): FEATURE FREEZE. NOTHING NEW ENTERS THE BUILD. REMAINING HOURS BELONG TO DEMO, VIDEO, PAGE.
H+36 — H+44PHASE 5 // CAPTURE THE EVIDENCE
  • Shoot the 30-second video per the Section 9 script: hook, live demo, impact. Two takes max; done beats perfect.
  • Finish the project page: mission statement, how-it-works, science paragraph, team roles, full data credits, the video embedded.
  • Record a 90-second full-quality backup walkthrough as insurance against venue wifi at local judging.
H+44 — H+48PHASE 6 // SHIP THE TRANSMISSION
  • Submit through the official portal with hours to spare. Verify: demo URL loads in an incognito browser, video plays, all required fields and team roster complete.
  • Run the smoke-test suite one final time; keep the app deployed through the local judging window.
  • Sleep before the local pitch if one exists. A rested team presenting five features beats an exhausted team presenting seven.
