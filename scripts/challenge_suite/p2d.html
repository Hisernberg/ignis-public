SECTION 07 // AWARD-LANE MAP
Which Trophies PYROCHRON Attacks

Aligned to the five judging criteria and the three Global Award categories in range.

Space Apps judges score Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, and Presentation, and global awards are category prizes. PYROCHRON is engineered so that each criterion has a named, demonstrable feature — there is no criterion where we improvise on stage. The primary lane is Best Use of Data: fusing two decades of two sensors with a published transfer function and third-party validation is precisely what that category exists to reward. The secondary lane is Global Connection: fire is a planetary phenomenon with regional faces, and the app is built for every continent by default, not as an afterthought. The tertiary lane is Best Use of Science, contested with #02’s PULSE in our own portfolio — PYROCHRON claims it whenever the validation panel lands strongly.
