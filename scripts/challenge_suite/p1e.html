SECTION 06 // THE VERDICT
Three Picks, One Engine, Distinct Trophies

The final selection — and the reserve bench with explicit swap triggers.

The matrix produces one flagship and two companions, chosen as a portfolio rather than as three independent favorites. The selection rule applied here: maximize award-lane coverage while minimizing stack change. All three picks run on the same Next.js + MapLibre + Python + Hugging Face chassis and share the same ingestion, time-series, statistics, and briefing layers; they differ only in the skin, the science module, and the story. That means the team can defer the final commitment to Hour 2 of the event, ping live data endpoints, watch which briefs the room crowds toward, and still lose nothing — because the engine is identical underneath.

06.1 The Picks
PICK 1 // FLAGSHIP — DOC SP-2026-003
#09 Harmonization of MODIS & VIIRS Hot Spots → PYROCHRON
Why it wins. Perfect data (FIRMS archive free and instant: MODIS to 2000, VIIRS 375 m to 20 Jan 2012), a bounded and honest science task (bias-correct two sensors into one calendar), a deliverable judges can verify in 90 seconds, and a user community — fire managers — that visibly needs it. Fires are a globalheadline industry: every judge has smoked air in their memory. Lanes: Best Use of Data, Global Connection, Best Use of Science.
PICK 2 // TWIN PIVOT — DOC SP-2026-004
#02 Earth System Trend Detective → PULSE
Why it wins. The scientific-rigor showcase: Mann-Kendall tests, Sen’s slope, and opposite-trend regional contrast, delivered on a catalog of free NASA variables (SST, NO₂, soil moisture, GRACE-FO water storage). Reuses ~70 percent of the PYROCHRON engine — if the fire brief gets crowded or FIRMS misbehaves, we pivot between the two with hours, not days, of work. Lanes: Best Use of Science, Best Use of Data.
PICK 3 // IMPACT ALTERNATE — DOC SP-2026-005
#07 Field Shift: Adapting Farms → ROOTLOGIC
Why it wins. The humanitarian lane nobody else in our stack can match as cleanly: NASA POWER + SMAP + GPM + SoilGrids fused into explainable crop-rotation guidance that visibly honors farmer priorities. It attacks Global Connection and Most Inspirational — trophies the fire and trend apps do not headline — and its quota-free data sources make it the safest fallback if satellite ingestion breaks on event weekend. If the team ever splits into two squads, #07 is the second squad’s designated brief.

SECTION 07 // PORTFOLIO STRATEGY
One Team, Two Squads, and the Shared Pre-Build

How the three picks convert into an execution decision — and what gets built before November 14.

Squad structure. With two to four people, the default is one team, one submission, pivot rights until Hour 2. Space Apps allows multiple teams per project — but a squad of two submitting to a second challenge sacrifices the depth that wins global awards; with four people, a 2+2 split is viable only if the second squad executes ROOTLOGIC’s pre-planned playbook rather than improvising. The recommended configuration: all hands on PYROCHRON, PULSE pre-staged as the pivot, ROOTLOGIC pre-staged for a split. Registration logistics are decided before the event — challenge submission is a per-project choice, and changing it later is a form field, not a rebuild, as long as the engine is shared.
