SECTION 05 // AI DIVISION & ARCHITECTURE
The LLM Explains; the Rules Decide

The division of authority is the architecture’s headline.

ROOTLOGIC’s AI posture inverts the usual hackathon pattern: the rule engine decides, the LLM explains. The agronomy rules are deterministic and auditable; the language model’s job is to translate rule traces into the farmer’s language (literally — multilingual output is a one-model feature), answer follow-up questions in a grounded chat (“why did cowpea lose points?”), and assemble the notebook. Retrieval grounding comes from USDA and FAO rotation guidance documents embedded with MiniLM; the model is instructed to refuse claims not present in the rule output or the corpus, and every chat answer carries its citation. This design survives both judge questions — “is AI doing real work?” (grounded multilingual advisory) and “can it hallucinate?” (nothing it says can outrank the rule trace).

SECTION 06 // LANES & PREP TIMELINE
Trophy Targets and the Weekend-Slot Schedule

ROOTLOGIC prep shares the flagship’s ten weeks and consumes its own weekend slots.

Award lanes. Primary: Global Connection — a tool conceived for the Sahel and the Midwest on the same screen, in the farmer’s language, on NASA’s free data. Secondary: Most Inspirational — the pitch closes on stewardship across generations, which this brief uniquely licenses. Tertiary: Best Use of Data in any timeline where the provenance-dense dossier shines. Criteria mapping: impact = farmer-facing utility; creativity = priorities-as-visible-physics sliders; validity = cited rule set and USDA/FAO grounding; relevance = every brief keyword made operational; presentation = the slider drag that reorders a farmer’s future.

Prep timeline. Shared weeks carry the shell and services (W1–2, W6–8 per SP-2026-003). ROOTLOGIC-specific slots: W4 — POWER/SoilGrids connectors and the two worked-region dossiers; W5 — rule engine v1 with ten rules and trace output; crop catalog v1 (12 crops with cited traits); W9 — sliders UI, notebook screen, grounded chat, multilingual sample (English + French + Hausa strings for the Sahel demo), offline bundle. The degraded mode mirrors the portfolio: if only one region is ready, the demo deepens rather than widens — a single-region tool with impeccable sourcing still clears every criterion.
