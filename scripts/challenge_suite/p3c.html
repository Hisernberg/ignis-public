SECTION 06 // LANES & PREP TIMELINE
Trophy Targets and the Compressed Schedule

PULSE prep runs inside the flagship’s ten weeks — it adds three items, not ten.

Award lanes. Primary: Best Use of Science — the named statistical protocol with displayed negative results is the strongest claim in the portfolio. Secondary: Best Use of Data, contested with the flagship; PULSE claims it in any timeline where six NASA variable families are fused with provenance panels. Tertiary: Global Connection via contrast pairs spanning hemispheres. The judging criteria map one-to-one: validity = protocol page; relevance = DAAC-linked catalog; impact = plain-language verdicts and projection; creativity = the Contrast Story; presentation = the two-verdict pitch close.

Prep timeline. Weeks 1–2 and 6–8 are shared with the flagship by design (shell, trend engine, AI services — already in SP-2026-003’s schedule). PULSE-specific additions: W4 — two connectors (SST, sea ice) live end-to-end, verdict endpoint returning MK/Sen/STL with pre-whitening flag; W5 — remaining four connectors; contrast scanner v1 on preset pairs; W9 — verdict panel UI polish, methodology page written with formulas and references, offline bundle validated. If the flagship consumes all Week 4–5 capacity, PULSE falls back to a three-variable build (SST, sea ice, soil moisture) — still a complete submission, still six screens of demo value from three families.

RESOURCE LAW
PULSE may never steal hours from the flagship’s harmonization engine before Week 6. It feeds on shared modules and its own weekend slots. The pivot must be free to remain a credible threat.
